Frequently asked questions

How do I enquire about a car?

Open Inventory, pick a car and click Enquire. Your message reaches our team.

How is the EMI calculated?

EMI uses your loan amount (price minus down payment), the yearly interest rate and the tenure you choose. It is an estimate, final terms come from your lender.

Can I contact a dealer directly?

Yes. The Dealer page lists each dealership with contact options.

Do I need an account?

No. Sign in to keep your details handy, but browsing and enquiries are open to everyone.
